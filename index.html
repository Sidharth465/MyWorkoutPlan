2. Goals

  • Lose fat around the stomach and love handles
  • Gain strength with progressive overload
  • Build a more muscular physique (body recomposition)
Note: Spot reduction does not work. Fat loss is systemic. Abs and love handles improve when overall body fat drops while muscle is preserved/built through lifting + protein.

Habits

  • Track food for 2–3 weeks to stay consistent
  • Aim for ~0.25–0.5 kg fat loss per week
  • If weight stalls for 2+ weeks, reduce calories by ~150–200
  • Limit sugary drinks, late-night snacking, and excess oils/nuts
  • Include protein in every meal (chicken, eggs, fish, dal, paneer, yogurt, whey)

9. How to Progress

  • Log every workout (exercise, weight, reps)
  • When you hit the top of the rep range on all sets with good form, add a little weight next time
  • Leave about 1–3 reps in reserve — do not fail every set
  • Stay consistent for at least 8 weeks before changing the program
  • Expect visible midsection changes in about 8–16 weeks with diet + training

10. Weekly Consistency Checklist

  • Protein every meal
  • Lift 4–6 days with this written plan
  • Walk daily (aim 8–10k steps)
  • Sleep 7–8 hours
  • Weigh yourself weekly (use a weekly average, not daily panic)

11. Extra Notes

  • If very tired after lifting, skip post-workout cardio and just hit your step goal that day
  • 1–2 longer cardio sessions can be done on rest days instead of after lifting
  • Keep post-lift cardio easy to moderate so recovery stays good
  • This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice. See a doctor if you have health conditions before starting.

Stay consistent. Strength first. Fat loss follows.
