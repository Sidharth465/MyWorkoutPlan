Personal Fitness Plan

Complete 6-Day Periodized Push / Pull / Legs Architecture (A/B Split)

Prepared for Sid  |  Full-Week Muscle Group Coverage

How This A/B System Works:
  • Push A vs. Push B: Push A focuses on chest, shoulders, and arms with presses, dips, and pushdowns. Push B adds an incline press, side raises, and overhead arm extensions.
  • Pull A vs. Pull B: Pull A emphasizes back width and arms. Pull B adds back thickness, rear-shoulder work, and hammer curls.
  • Legs A vs. Legs B: Legs A emphasizes thighs and core. Legs B pairs hamstrings and glutes with calves and core work.

8. Progression & Nutrition Rules

  • Double Progression: Start at the low rep target (e.g. 8 reps). Keep that weight until you can complete the high rep limit (e.g. 12 reps) across all sets with strict form before increasing weight by 1–2 kg on dumbbells or 2.5–5 kg on machines/bars.
  • RIR (Reps in Reserve): Maintain 1–2 reps in reserve on compound lifts (bench presses, squats, rows, RDLs). Take isolation exercises (curls, pushdowns, lateral raises, leg extensions) to 0–1 RIR.
  • Rest Intervals: Rest 2–3 minutes on heavy compound lifts; 60–90 seconds on isolation and core exercises.
  • Daily Nutrition: 1,900–2,100 kcal/day (mild cut for fat loss around midsection), 130–160 g protein daily, and 3–4 liters of water.

Stay consistent. Strength first. Fat loss follows.
